## Using the Application

1. **Ask questions** about US Census demographics in natural language
   - "What is the population of California?"
   - "Show me population breakdown by age in Texas"
   - "How many seniors (65+) are in the USA?"

2. **Quick Links** - Click example questions at the top to auto-populate and submit

3. **Model Suggestions** - Click suggested questions from the model to explore further

4. **Chat History** - Multi-turn conversations preserve context automatically

## Features

- 🤖 Natural language queries powered by Snowflake Cortex Analyst
- 📊 Real-time demographic data from US Census
- ✅ 4-layer validation ensures data quality
- 🔄 Conversation context preservation
- ⚡ Quick links for exploratory queries
- 📈 Comprehensive test coverage (126+ tests)

## Data Available

- **Population by Age & Sex** - All 50 states, counties, and block groups
- **Geography Levels** - State, County, Census Block Group
- **Coverage** - 2020 US Census data
